How this version works

1

We measure and centre the STL, then check there is enough room for the chosen dice.

2

A reinforced internal chute is placed through the middle so the dice path does not depend on the downloaded model's internal geometry.

3

The tray is attached to the chosen front. The finished STL is checked against the Neptune 3 Pro's build volume before it is returned.

Prototype rule: preview converted towers before printing. Very thin, hollow or strongly tapered decorative STLs may need the next version's movable chute controls.
